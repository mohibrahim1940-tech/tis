Pipeline commissioning sequence

Eight steps from mechanical completion to first flow.

Every TIS commissioning project follows this sequence. Each step is signed off before the next begins, and the records go into the handover dossier.

  1. 01

    Mechanical completion

    Walkdowns, punch-list closure and verification against as-built drawings.

  2. 02

    Cleaning & gauging

    Pigging runs to remove construction debris and confirm bore with a gauging plate.

  3. 03

    Hydrostatic testing

    Pressure test held for the specified period to prove strength and leak tightness.

  4. 04

    Dewatering

    Displacement of test water by pig trains and safe disposal of the water.

  5. 05

    Drying

    Air or vacuum drying until the line meets the specified dew point.

  6. 06

    Nitrogen purging

    Inerting the line to remove oxygen before hydrocarbons are introduced.

  7. 07

    Pre-start-up review

    PSSR covering instruments, safety systems, metering and operating procedures.

  8. 08

    Line packing & first flow

    Controlled introduction of product, stabilisation and handover to operations.

Our investigation process

Six steps from the event to a closed-out action.

Every investigation follows the same sequence, so findings are traceable and the corrective actions are checked to be working.

  1. 01

    Preserve evidence

    Secure failed parts, data, logs and records before they are lost or altered.

  2. 02

    Define the problem

    Agree what happened, its impact and the scope of the investigation.

  3. 03

    Build the timeline

    Reconstruct the sequence of events from data, inspections and interviews.

  4. 04

    Analyse causes

    Work from symptoms to physical, human and organisational root causes.

  5. 05

    Corrective actions

    Set actions with owners and deadlines that remove each root cause.

  6. 06

    Verify & close out

    Confirm the actions are in place and the failure has not returned.
